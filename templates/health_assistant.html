Veterinary & Medical Disclaimer

The PawCare Health Assistant is an informational, organizational, and educational resource intended to assist pet parents in recording observations and preparing for consultations with veterinary professionals. It is never a substitute for clinical veterinary diagnosis, in-person examination, or emergency medical treatment. If your pet exhibits sudden lethargy, respiratory distress, seizures, suspected poisoning, or traumatic injuries, immediately contact a licensed veterinarian or a 24-hour emergency animal hospital.
