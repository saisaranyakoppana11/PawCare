How PawCare AI Analysis Works

Behind the scenes of our multi-stage deep learning pipeline

1
Image Preprocessing

The uploaded image is converted to RGB format, resized to 224x224 pixels, and prepared for deep learning model inference.

2
Species Detection

Dedicated Cat and Dog detector models determine whether the uploaded image belongs to a supported cat or dog before breed classification.

3
Breed Classification

The appropriate breed classifier analyzes the detected animal and returns the most likely breed class along with alternative predictions and confidence scores.
